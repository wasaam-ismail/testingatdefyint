Our story

Made for the memories you will want to return to.

Few of us will sit down to watch a two-hour wedding film. Most of us will reach for our phones a hundred times this year.

Bride's Best Friend began with that simple thought. Social media has become the scrapbook of our time, so your fondest memories should live there too, ready to replay on a quiet evening or share with someone who wishes they could have been in the room.

On the day itself, couples are often swept up in tradition, ceremony and the pressure of everything running on time. We are there to notice what you might miss: the spark, the tenderness and the honest emotion woven through every ritual. When the reels arrive, you get to see just how beautiful it all was.

The BBF way

Like having your best friend behind the camera.

i.

Friendly

Your comfort comes first. Brides and their girls are at ease from the first hello, and when the spotlight turns to the groom's side, we are right there too.

ii.

Flexible

We read the room and move with your day, never against it. Packages bend to your plans, from an intimate dinner to a three-day celebration.

iii.

Calm, discreet and quietly organised, so you can stop managing the moment and start living it. Emotion first, trends only when they truly suit you.

How it works

From first hello to forever on repeat.

  1. Say hello

    Share your date and where you are celebrating. Availability and location are all we need to begin.

  2. Discovery call

    We talk through your events, your people and the moments you most want to keep.

  3. Your package

    Choose your shooting hours and number of reels, or build something bespoke across every event.

  4. The day

    We blend in, read the room and capture the emotion, so you can stay present with the people you love.

  5. Your reels

    Every reel is edited personally by Fareeda. Quality always comes before speed, especially for multi-day celebrations.

Your privacy is sacred. For families with cultural, political or personal reasons to stay private, nothing is posted or shared without your blessing. Discretion is part of every package.

Good to know

Questions couples often ask.

Anything else on your mind? Send us a message on WhatsApp and we will reply personally.

What are your package rates?

Every package is built from two things: the number of shooting hours and the number of reels you would like. Share your date, venue and events, and we will send a tailored quote along with our curated packages.

Are you available on my date?

Dates fill quickly during wedding season, so reach out as early as you can. Message us your date and location and we will confirm availability right away.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes. We cover celebrations across Sri Lanka and love travelling overseas for destination weddings. Travel details are planned together and included in your quote.

How long until I receive my reels?

Every reel is edited personally, and we will always choose a beautiful reel over a rushed one. Timelines depend on the size of your celebration; a three-day wedding naturally takes a little longer. We will agree on a delivery plan before your day.

Can I combine different services?

Absolutely. Mix reels, images, pre-wedding and event coverage into one personalised package, as long as the deliverables are practical for your schedule.

Will my content be posted publicly?

Only with your permission. Many of our couples love sharing their reels, and some prefer to keep things private. Either way, your wishes come first.
