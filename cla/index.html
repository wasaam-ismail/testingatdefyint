Design & Presence

Sculpted to be noticed.
Engineered to perform.

Frameless doors. A sculpted shark-nose silhouette. A signature light front and rear. Every line on the new CLA has a purpose — and the effect is unmistakable.

01 — Signature Face

The illuminated star grille.

The radiator grille is illuminated in the iconic Mercedes-Benz pattern, with contour lighting that glows after dark. Lock or unlock the CLA and it greets — and bids farewell — with its own light animation.

  • Illuminated Mercedes-Benz pattern grille in chrome
  • MULTIBEAM LED headlamps with adaptive lighting
  • Welcome & farewell light choreography
02 — Rear Signature

A taillight you'd know anywhere.

A continuous LED light band traces the width of the boot lid, anchored by the star-patterned taillight graphic — a silhouette that stays recognisable from a block away, day or night.

  • Full-width LED light band
  • Star-pattern taillight graphic
  • Frameless doors for an uninterrupted silhouette
03 — Stance & Wheels

Poise, from every angle.

Aerodynamically optimised light-alloy wheels — up to 19 inches on AMG Line — are tuned for both drag and drama, finished in high-gloss black, black sheen and dark platinum gloss.

Intelligence, Reimagined

A cabin that thinks
ahead of you.

Step inside and the new CLA's cabin feels less like a car interior and more like a private lounge — wrapped around three screens, dozens of ambient hues, and a sky full of glass.

04 — MBUX Superscreen

Three screens. One seamless experience.

A 10.25" driver display with individually configurable contexts sits beside a 14" central touch display with a zero-layer interface — with the optional MBUX Superscreen adding a 14" front passenger display for films, apps and games, invisible from the driver's seat.

  • 10.25" driver display, 2 configurable contexts
  • 14" central display, touch input, zero-layer UI
  • 14" front passenger display (MBUX Superscreen)
  • MBUX Navigation — to find your way
  • Apple CarPlay® & Android Auto™ integration
05 — Ambient Lighting

64 colours. A mood for every mile.

Ambient lighting washes the instrument panel, centre console and door trim in your choice of 64 colours across 10 curated schemes — adjustable across 20 brightness levels and 3 independent dimming zones.

  • 64 colours across 10 colour schemes
  • 20 brightness levels, 3 dimming zones
  • Synced to drive modes & welcome sequence
06 — Panoramic Roof

Bring the sky in.

The panoramic glass roof spans from the windscreen to the rear, flooding the cabin in light. Special coatings filter UV and heat, so the openness never comes at the cost of comfort.

  • Glass module extends to the rear windscreen
  • UV & heat-reducing coatings
07 — Confidence, Built In

Assistance that watches the road, not you.

From adaptive DISTRONIC cruise control to parking assist with a reversing camera and front/rear sensors, the new CLA's assistance suite is engineered to make every drive calmer — with facial recognition and a built-in selfie & video camera for a genuinely personal cabin.

  • Adaptive Distance Assist DISTRONIC
  • MB.DRIVE Parking Assist + reversing camera
  • Blind Spot Assist
  • Facial recognition & selfie/video camera
  • Wireless smartphone charging (front)
