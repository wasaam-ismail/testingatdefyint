2. What we collect

When you send the test-drive form: your full name, email address, contact number, WhatsApp number, current vehicle, preferred option (cash, trade-in or lease), whether you would like to receive news and offers, and the date and time of your enquiry and consent.

When you use the page: only if you accept cookies, our analytics and advertising partners collect information such as the pages and sections you view, buttons you tap, the device and browser you use, your approximate location and your IP address. See section 8.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, arrange your test drive and prepare a personalised quote, including trade-in and lease options.
  • To follow up with you by phone, WhatsApp or email about your enquiry.
  • Only if you tick the optional box: to send you news and offers about Mercedes-Benz vehicles and services from DIMO.
  • Only if you accept cookies: to understand how the page is used and to measure and improve our advertising on Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram).

4. Our legal basis

We rely on your consent: the consent box on the form, the optional marketing box, and your cookie choice. You can withdraw any of these at any time, without affecting anything we did before you withdrew. To change your cookie choice, use Cookie settings.

5. Who we share it with

  • DIMO’s Mercedes-Benz product specialists and sales team.

  • Service providers who host this website and store or process enquiries for us [CRM / FORM PROVIDER], under contracts that require them to protect your data.

We do not sell your personal data.
