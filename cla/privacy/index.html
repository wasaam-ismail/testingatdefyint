Privacy & Cookie Notice

The all-new Mercedes-Benz CLA page · Last updated 13 November 2026

This is a supplementary notice for the all-new Mercedes-Benz CLA campaign page. It explains what this page collects and why. It adds to DIMO’s Privacy Notice, which describes in full how DIMO handles personal data and your rights: read DIMO’s Privacy Notice (PDF).

1. Who we are

This page is run by Diesel & Motor Engineering PLC (“DIMO”, “we”, “us”), a company incorporated in Sri Lanka, company registration number PQ 146, with its registered office at No. 65, Jethawana Road, Colombo 14, Sri Lanka. DIMO decides how and why the personal data collected through this page is used, and is responsible for it under Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 (PDPA).

Privacy questions: contact our Data Protection Officer at DPO@dimolanka.com, or call 0112 449 797.

2. What we collect

When you send the test-drive form: your full name, email address, contact number, WhatsApp number (if different), current vehicle, the model you are interested in, your preferred financing option, whether you would like to receive news and offers, and the date and time of your enquiry and consent. The form also records the address of this page and any campaign tags in the link that brought you here (for example, which advert you clicked).

If you message us on WhatsApp: your WhatsApp number, profile name and the messages you send.

When you use the page: only if you accept cookies, our analytics and advertising partners collect information such as the sections you view, buttons you tap, the device and browser you use, your approximate location and your IP address. See section 8.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, arrange your test drive and prepare a personalised quote, including trade-in and lease options.
  • To follow up with you by phone, WhatsApp or email about your enquiry.
  • Only if you answer “Yes” to the optional news and offers question: to send you news and offers about Mercedes-Benz vehicles and services from DIMO.
  • Only if you accept cookies: to understand how the page is used and to measure and improve our advertising on Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram).

4. Our legal basis

We rely on your consent: the consent box on the form, your answer to the optional news and offers question, and your cookie choice. You can withdraw any of these at any time, without affecting anything we did before you withdrew. To change your cookie choice, use Cookie settings.

5. Who we share it with

  • DIMO’s Mercedes-Benz product specialists and sales team, on a need-to-know basis.
  • Our partner banks and leasing companies, only if you ask for a finance or lease quote, so they can prepare it.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contracts that require them to keep it confidential and secure: the company that hosts this website, and Sprout (hellosprout.ai), the platform DIMO uses to receive and manage enquiries from this form and WhatsApp conversations.
  • Google reCAPTCHA, which checks that the form is being sent by a person and not an automated program. Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms apply.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to message us on WhatsApp.
  • Google and Meta, only if you accept analytics or advertising cookies.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Data processed outside Sri Lanka

Some of our service providers, including Google and Meta, may process data outside Sri Lanka. Such transfers only take place to a country approved by the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka, or where we can still meet our obligations under the PDPA and have put in place an appropriate instrument prescribed by the Authority, or as the PDPA otherwise permits.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected for: to handle your enquiry and test drive and, if you go ahead, to complete your purchase and deal with any questions or complaints afterwards. If you opted in to news and offers, we keep your contact details for that purpose until you opt out. We regularly review the data we hold and securely delete or anonymise it once there is no longer a legal, business or customer need to keep it.

9. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold your personal data and give you access to it;
  • correct it if it is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete it, or stop or limit how we use it;
  • stop sending you marketing, at any time;
  • withdraw your consent.

Contact our Data Protection Officer at DPO@dimolanka.com or call 0112 449 797. We will reply within the time required by law. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.

10. Security

This page and the enquiry form are served over secure (HTTPS) connections. Access to your data is limited to staff and service providers who need it, and they are bound by confidentiality obligations.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. For everything not covered here, DIMO’s Privacy Notice applies.
