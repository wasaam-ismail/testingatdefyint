Jeep Wrangler Rubicon

The icon. Unfinished business.

It never needed to change. So it simply got better at everything.

Seven slots. Two round eyes. A bloodline that goes back to 1941.

The Wrangler is the one everyone recognises from a kilometre away. Rubicon is the one that goes further than anyone else once they get there: lockers, Dana axles and a 4:1 low range, wrapped around a cabin you'll happily commute in.

Made for the outdoor commute.

Weekday-ready, weekend-proven. A 12.3-inch Uconnect® touchscreen with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, Alpine® premium audio, and Nappa leather seats with 8-way power adjustment. Monday has never looked this good.

  • 12.3" touchscreen and 7.0" colour cluster
  • Keyless-Go and auto temperature control
  • Power-heated mirrors, ambient LED lighting

Trail Rated. Straight from the showroom.

When the tarmac runs out, the Rubicon is only getting started. Dana M210 and M220 axles, electronic lockers front and rear, a disconnecting front sway bar and Off-Road+ mode, all on 255/75R17 BFGoodrich® tyres.

  • Selec-Speed® control and off-road switch bank
  • Rock rails plus transfer case, fuel tank and transmission skid plates
  • 4.10 rear axle ratio

Open air, on your terms.

Lift off the three-piece hard top and the sky becomes part of the cabin. Take the doors off and the drive becomes part of you. Put it all back when the monsoon arrives.

  • Black three-piece hard top with Freedom Panel storage bag
  • Side-curtain air bags for front and rear passengers
  • Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop & Go, Forward Collision Warning Plus

Jeep Gladiator Rubicon

All Jeep. Plus room for the rest.

The grille, the freedom and the capability, with a proper cargo bed out back.

Bikes, boards, the camping kit and the cooler. Bring it all.

The Gladiator is what happens when the world's most capable SUV gets a pickup bed and a 3.6-litre V6. Trail Rated where it counts, comfortable where you spend most of your week, and ready to haul the weekend wherever it's going.

Load it. Tow it. Go.

Up to 3,175 kg on the hitch and 782 kg in the bed. The boat, the dirt bikes, the whole crew's gear for a week up-country: it's all coming with you.

  • Trailer Sway Damping and Hill Start Assist
  • Red tow hooks and steel rear bumper
  • Body-colour three-piece hard top and fender flares

Rock-Trac. Rock solid.

4:1 Rock-Trac® full-time 4WD, Dana M210 and M220 axles and E-Lockers front and rear. A disconnecting front sway bar lets the wheels find grip where other pickups find excuses.

  • Off-road camera and ParkSense® rear park assist
  • Skid plates for transfer case, fuel tank and transmission
  • Electronic Roll Mitigation and Stability Control

Your soundtrack, wherever you park.

Alpine® premium audio with subwoofer inside, and a wireless Bluetooth® speaker you can take to the campsite. 12.3 inches of Uconnect® up front, with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto to plan the next detour.

  • Nappa leather, 8-way power front seats
  • Integrated voice command
  • LED headlamps, fog lamps and tail lamps
